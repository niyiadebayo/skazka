# Skazka Tours — Visual Storytelling System

## Read PHILOSOPHY.md for the why

`PHILOSOPHY.md` holds the design philosophy: the idea, the beliefs, the signature (the arc, the field, the lit index, large numerals), the colour names, the floors and what we refuse. This file is the how. Where the two disagree, PHILOSOPHY.md wins and this file is out of date.

**Signature layer (November 2026).** The last block of `src/site.css`, headed SIGNATURE, implements it:
- the arc mark (`#i-arc` in the sprite; `assets/arc.svg` for the browser tab and chat),
- `--field` laterite `#6E2B16` and `--brass` `#D4B272` (these replaced ember and apricot),
- `.running` heads, `.page-title` openings, `.statement`, `.split` closing cards, `.lit-index` in the footer,
- labels in sentence case at 14px. The label rule is generated from every selector that used to be set in capitals; if you add a new label style, do not use `text-transform: uppercase`.

## How the site is built (read this first, November 2026)

The site is seven pages generated by `build.py` from the files in `src/`. The pages in the repository root (`index.html`, `journeys/index.html` and so on) are output: do not edit them by hand.

```
python3 build.py     # after any change in src/
```

| Edit this | For |
|---|---|
| `src/content.py` | Journeys, regions, questions, guides and languages (text and data) |
| `src/sections/*.html` | Hero, day in the field, founder, method steps, house rules, guest letter, enquiry form |
| `src/parts/*.html` | Menu, footer, chat, the silhouette drawings |
| `src/site.css`, `src/site.js` | Look and behaviour, shared by every page |
| `build.py` | Page list, titles and descriptions, page layouts, structured data, sitemap |

- **Pages:** `/` (hero, a day in the field, teasers, guest letter), `/journeys/`, `/regions/`, `/guides/`, `/method/` (steps and house rules), `/questions/`, `/enquire/`. `404.html` stays hand-written and self-contained.
- **Assets are fingerprinted.** The stylesheet and script are written as `assets/site.<hash>.css` and `.js`, because `/assets/*` is cached for a year. The build removes the previous ones.
- **Old links still work.** `skazka.tours/#enquire` and the other single-page anchors are sent on by a few lines at the top of `site.js`; `/about`, `/faq` and `/contact` by `_redirects`.
- **A chosen journey travels in the address:** `/enquire/?journey=...` puts it into the enquiry note.
- **Structured data per page**, generated from `content.py`: the agency on the home page, the three journeys on `/journeys/`, the FAQ on `/questions/`, Kimani on `/guides/`, breadcrumbs on every inner page.
- `src/` and `build.py` are listed in `.assetsignore`, so they are not published.

**No italics, anywhere.** No italic styles, no italic fonts requested, and no `<em>`, `<i>` or `<cite>` tags in the markup, so nothing slants even in reader mode or without the stylesheet. Do not add them back. Headlines carry no visual emphasis.

**Guides and languages.** `content.py` lists five people. Kimani and Elena are real. The Japanese-, Korean- and Spanish-speaking guides are placeholders (`placeholder=True`): their cards say "Profile to come" and carry no invented names. The hero and footer list all six languages because that was asked for; the structured data and `llms.txt` still list only English, Swahili and Russian, and should stay that way until those guides exist. Parts of this document below describe the earlier single-page site.

## Direction

The site is now structured as a visual narrative instead of a standard landing page.
Each locale follows the same arc:

1. **Opening hook** — emotional invitation and visual promise
2. **Story setup** — how the trip is shaped around feeling and pace
3. **Journey movement** — a day-in-the-life rhythm section
4. **Audience fit** — different traveler archetypes
5. **Planning arc** — how the process unfolds
6. **Practical clarity** — cost, scope, flexibility, safety
7. **Closing chapter** — direct contact CTA

## Direction: quiet luxury, legible (November 2026)

Researched against Aman (warm neutrals, colour only from photographs, an editorial serif with a quiet sans, square corners, generous space) and Gentle Monster (gallery presentation, negative space, thin rules). The first pass was too faint; a rendered-page audit found body copy at 4.7:1, 11.5px labels, and hero text at 2-4:1 over the sky. These floors now apply.

**Legibility floors (measured on the rendered page, not assumed)**
- Copy is `--ink` (13:1 or better on every ground). Labels and captions are `--ink-dim` (8:1 or better).
- Nothing under 12.5px. No font weight under 400.
- Hero and nav text: 6.9:1 or better against the actual video pixels (worst case, brightest 10% behind each line).
- The accent is for marks (dots, rules, the progress line), never for copy.
- Emphasis in headlines is italic only, same colour.

**Principles**
- **Space is material.** Sections carry 112-192px of padding.
- **Colour comes from the photographs.** The interface stays linen to near-black.
- **Hairlines you can see.** Borders at 20-50% ink; square corners.
- **Gradients as light.** Two close stops only (the day moments); the hero scrim darkens the left third and the land, and leaves the sun alone. The scrim must sit above the video (`z-index: 2`).
- **Slow, sparse motion.** 0.6s eased transitions. Nothing starts hidden.

**Hero**
- Desktop: words on the left over a scrim; the four facts are a bottom strip on the dark land.
- Phone: the picture fills the top 56vh, and the words sit beneath it on solid ground.

**Small delights**
- The hero line and the day chapter follow the Kenya clock ("16:00 in Kenya · the afternoon drive is out").
- A 2px reading-progress line at the top (CSS scroll timeline; absent where unsupported).
- Underlines that draw in on links; a tick on chosen chips; the enquiry note brightens briefly when it rewrites itself.

## Flow, navigation and storybook motion (November 2026)

Read as a parent planning a family trip. Skazka means "fairy tale", so the illustration is storybook silhouette. All drawings are original inline SVG (`#i-acacia`, `#i-elephant`, `#i-giraffe`, `#i-jeep`); no characters or art from any film or book.

**Chapter order** (feel, then facts, then trust, then action)
1. A day in the field 2. Sample journeys 3. Regions 4. Who leads your trip 5. The method 6. House rules 7. In their words 8. Questions 9. Begin

The hero and chapter 1 share the night ground, so the opening reads as one act. After that, linen and sand alternate, and the page ends on a dusk scene that runs into a dark footer.

**Navigation**
- Desktop: six links; the chapter being read is underlined (IntersectionObserver, class only).
- Phones: a `<details>` menu with all nine chapters plus WhatsApp and email. It works without JavaScript.
- A small safari vehicle drives along the bottom edge of the nav as far as the reader has scrolled.
- The chat collapses to a square mark once reading starts, and hides while the phone menu is open.

**Motion**
- Hero words arrive in order on load (CSS only, finishes by itself).
- The sun crosses an arc above the day cards by the Kenya clock; at night a moon and stars take over.
- Blocks settle 40px into place as they enter (CSS `view()` timeline, transform only; absent where unsupported).
- Birds drift in the closing scene. Everything above stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Animal tracks** mark each chapter label: lion, zebra, elephant, antelope, guineafowl (CSS masks, `data-track`).

## Sample journeys: three spreads (November 2026)

The card grid was replaced because it read like a shop. Each journey is now a spread, like facing pages: a 3:2 photograph on one side and the telling on the other, alternating sides, with white space between them and no boxes.

- Label (`Journey № 01 — 12 days`), serif title, one paragraph, a route, then price and one link.
- The route is a dotted trail of the places named in that journey's description, with a small vehicle that travels it as the spread scrolls past (or on hover where scroll timelines are unsupported). Kimani should confirm the stops for Mara Classic and the coast journey.
- Three journeys: Mara Classic (10 days), bush and coast (14 days), and the short trip (5 days). The Great Rift and Samburu & Laikipia were dropped from the page in November 2026; their regions remain in the Regions chapter.
- "Small party, two to six" is said once in the intro, not on every journey.
- The whole spread is one link to the enquiry form; choosing it adds the journey to the enquiry note and marks the spread "In your note".
- Phones: photo above, text below.

## Hero by the Kenya clock (November 2026)

The hero changes with Kenya time (UTC+3), the same clock as the "in Kenya now" line.

| Phase | Kenya time | Today |
|---|---|---|
| dawn | 05:30 - 08:00 | sunset clip, tinted cooler and pinker |
| day | 08:00 - 16:30 | a still photograph (`assets/hero-day.jpg`, Unsplash stock: an elephant in golden grass), drifting very slowly |
| sunset | 16:30 - 19:00 | the clip as filmed |
| night | 19:00 - 05:30 | sunset clip turned moonlit blue |

Only one clip exists, so three phases are tints (CSS filter plus a colour wash on `.hero-bg::before`). Without JavaScript the hero shows as filmed.

**Adding real footage for a phase**
1. Add `assets/hero-<phase>.mp4` and `assets/hero-poster-<phase>.jpg` (phase is `dawn`, `day` or `night`).
2. Add the phase to `data-clips` on the hero video, e.g. `data-clips="sunset dawn"`.

That phase then plays its own clip and its tint switches off. A phase can be a still photograph instead: add `assets/hero-<phase>.jpg` and list the phase in `data-stills`. The list is explicit on purpose: probing for files would make every visitor request clips that do not exist, and `/assets/*` is cached for a year.

Hero text contrast is measured per phase against the video pixels; the lowest is the 88px headline at 4.9:1 in the day phase, and all small text is 7.7:1 or better.

## Stock photographs: look before you label (November 2026)

Until November 2026 nobody had looked at the Unsplash photos on this page, and most did not show what their alt text said (a "balloon over acacia" was a hiker on a hilltop; "giraffe, Samburu" was the Earth from the stratosphere; one link was dead). They were replaced with photos that were downloaded and viewed first.

- Never add an Unsplash ID from memory. Download it and look at it.
- Alt text describes what is in the picture. It does not claim a Kenyan location for a stock photo.
- Current stock IDs: zebras `1518709594023`, balloon `1519659528534`, wildebeest and vehicle `1535082623926`, rhino and calf `1535338454770`, giraffe at dusk `1523805009345`, acacia over red earth `1547471080`, founder placeholder `1732027198077`, daytime hero `1535941339077` (saved locally).

## Search and AI visibility (November 2026)

**Rule: structured data repeats the page, nothing more.** The JSON-LD FAQ used to hold four questions that were not on the page and contradicted it (a $3,000-$8,000 price range, a cancellation policy, an air-ambulance partner). The FAQ, the three journeys (`TouristTrip` with from-prices) and the agency facts in the JSON-LD are now generated from the visible HTML. When the page copy changes, regenerate them.

- Title 56 characters, description 157; social tags match.
- One h1; headings never skip a level (footer and chat labels are paragraphs, not headings).
- Three questions added to the visible FAQ because people ask them of search engines and AI assistants: cost, Russian-speaking guides, best time to go. Each answer restates facts already on the page.
- `robots.txt` names the main AI crawlers as welcome. `llms.txt` is the plain-text fact sheet for them; the page links to it. Keep `llms.txt`, `skazka.md`, the JSON-LD and the page in step.
- "Now booking" in the hero is a rolling window, three to ten months ahead of the current month in Kenya. The rule was inferred from the old fixed dates; change it in the script if the real booking window differs.
- Not on the page and so not in the data: reviews, ratings, social profiles, a street address. Add them only when they are real.

## 404 page and what gets published (November 2026)

- `404.html` is self-contained (inline styles, root-relative links) so it works at any path depth. Same palette, type and dusk scene as the home page; the silhouettes are copied from `index.html`, so update both if the drawings change.
- `wrangler.jsonc` sets `not_found_handling: "404-page"`. Without it, Cloudflare Workers static assets answer unknown paths with an empty 404 and never show `404.html`.
- `.assetsignore` lists the repository files that are not part of the website (`DESIGN.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `docs/`, `wrangler.jsonc`). The asset directory is the repository root, so anything not listed there is public.
- `assets/site.css` and `assets/site.js` were the old 404 page's only dependencies and are gone.

## Layout Principles

- Max content width: `1180px`
- Hero is a two-column cinematic split on desktop, stacked on mobile
- Most chapters use a header row plus a card/grid layout
- Journey chapter shifts into a dark immersive band for contrast and pacing
- Closing CTA mirrors the hero with a final high-emotion panel

## Motion

- Reveal-on-scroll is powered by `IntersectionObserver` in `assets/site.js`
- `.reveal` elements animate upward into place
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables transitions and reveals

## Accessibility

- Skip link included on all pages
- Semantic landmarks preserved: `nav`, `main`, `header`, `section`, `footer`
- All CTA states have visible focus outlines
- All imagery uses descriptive `alt` text
- Motion is reduced automatically for users who request it
