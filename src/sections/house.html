the house rules. the promises we keep on every trip.

plans shift daily. these don't.

the rules

  1. One guide, the whole trip.The same person every day, from the first airport door to the last goodbye. You will know who it is before you arrive.
  2. Small parties only.Two, four or six of you. One vehicle, one guide, one chef. We do not pool strangers. Ever.
  3. The itinerary is a starting point.If the leopard cubs come back, we stay another night.
  4. Route around the storm.If a storm cuts a road, we find another way instead of cancelling a day. Redundancy on vehicles, power and water.
  5. Explain the land.How the Maasai built their enkaji to breathe. How the railway laid in 1899 redrew the migrations you will see.
  6. Leave room to do nothing.Rest days are built in. Many trips finish in Lamu with three days of doing very little.
  7. Every enquiry, read personally.No sales team between us. A reply within two working days.

things we don't do

  • sell fixed packages
  • hand you between a dozen vendors
  • put your party in a vehicle with strangers
  • cancel a day because a road washed out
  • send a plan without a price and a reason for every choice
— Kimani Nairobi. If one of these is ever broken on your trip, tell me.
