"Every safari I run carries a quiet thread of engineering — how the Maasai built their enkaji to breathe, why a Turkana well is still cooler than yours will ever be, how the rail we laid in 1899 redrew every migration you will see today."

I founded Skazka in Nairobi in 2015. Trained as an electrical engineer at the Kazan Power Engineering University, I spent four years designing substations before I gave it up for the field. Ten-plus years later I hold a KPSGA silver license and lead a team of twenty-five — guides, chefs, mechanics, pilots, and our Russian-speaking manager Elena — who run trips with me.

I speak English, Swahili, and Russian, which now means I am the only guide in Kenya most of my Moscow guests will trust on a two-way radio. Our multilingual team carries the rest.
