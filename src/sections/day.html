A day
in the field.

You won't have a typical day. However, this is the rhythm most of them follow, set by the light and the weather of the day.

In Kenya now EAT
Kenya runs on East Africa Time, UTC+3, all year.

  5. 15:30 Now

    So close to the equator, it comes at almost the same time all year.

    And tomorrow's plan, adjusted to what today showed us.
